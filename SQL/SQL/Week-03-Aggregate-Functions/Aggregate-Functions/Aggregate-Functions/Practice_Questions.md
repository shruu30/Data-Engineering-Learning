# Week 3 - Aggregate Functions Practice Questions
---

## Question 1
Display the total number of employees.

**Concept:** COUNT()

---

## Question 2
Display the total salary of all employees.

**Concept:** SUM()

---

## Question 3
Display the average salary of employees.

**Concept:** AVG()

---

## Question 4
Display the highest and lowest salary.

**Concept:** MAX(), MIN()

---

## Question 5
Display the number of employees in each department.

**Concept:** GROUP BY

---

## Question 6
Display the total salary paid by each department.

**Concept:** SUM() + GROUP BY

---

## Question 7
Display departments where more than one employee works.

**Concept:** GROUP BY + HAVING

---

## Question 8
Display employees with a salary category using CASE WHEN:

- Salary >= 70000 → High
- Salary >= 50000 → Medium
- Salary < 50000 → Low

**Concept:** CASE WHEN

---

## Question 9
Display the average salary for each city.

**Concept:** AVG() + GROUP BY

---

## Question 10
Display departments whose average salary is greater than 60000.

**Concept:** AVG() + GROUP BY + HAVING
